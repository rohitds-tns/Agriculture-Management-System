Agriculture Management System

Agriculture management system gives an idea to the farmers to grow the crops and also to sell their products online. Agriculture management website application which helps the farmers to give best-practice of farming processes. Farmers will get the new ideas to improve their productivity and profitability. Farmers can buy and sell the seeds, pesticides, and tools through online as well as directly from the seller.
